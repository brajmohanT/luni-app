The supporting details

Repeat the same few decisions everywhere.

The Luni mark.

Use the supplied logo artwork. Preserve its proportions and white fill on blue or dark surfaces. Reserve blue texture for brand artwork.

Shape follows the content.

Reference-matched proposal: 12 px corners, 10 × 12 px padding, and a curved lower-right tail on outgoing messages. Incoming bubbles have no tail. Circular avatars; pill reactions and composer.

A rhythm of 4.

4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 px. Keep the name, message, and reactions close. Give different speakers more separation.

Chat component library

First pass · light and dark

Electric-blue outgoing messages, quiet incoming surfaces, and the supplied Luni identity. Review the component states below before applying them to app screens.

  • Message body: 16/22 regular. Names: 14/20 semibold. Metadata: 12/16 regular. Font family remains provisional.
  • Bubbles: 12 px corners, 10 × 12 px padding, up to 88% width. Only the last outgoing bubble in a group gets a tail.
  • Controls: at least 44 × 44 px. Composer grows to six lines, then scrolls. Keep messages readable when text size increases.
  • Sending and retry must preserve the original message. Distinguish “Sent” from “Read”; this demo makes no read-receipt claim.
  • Typing and streaming examples are static. Keep typing motion optional and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Shared controls & settings

Light and dark · interactive examples

Use the same typography, blue accent, and surface tokens across chat and settings. All interactions below are local demonstrations.

  • Use a filled blue button for the main action, an outlined button for alternatives, and a labeled destructive button for resets or removal.
  • Inputs keep visible labels. Show errors in text as well as color, and preserve entered values.
  • Rows with chevrons open another choice or view. Switches apply immediately; state changes are announced.
  • Dialogs keep keyboard focus inside while open. Escape cancels; closing returns focus to the trigger.
